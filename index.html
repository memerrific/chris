Our approach

No hand-offs, no strangers on site. Just one team who care how it turns out.

Most bathrooms pass through four or five different trades. Ours never leave our hands. The people who plan your room are the same people who prepare the walls, set every tile and fit the last tap, so nothing is lost between stages and the finish is exactly as we drew it.

What we do

Considered from the first sketch to the final detail

Design

We visit, listen and measure, then help you choose a layout, tiles and fittings that suit your home and how you use the room.

Preparation

Walls boarded true, floors levelled and every wet area waterproofed. Large-format tiles go up with levelling clips so every face sits perfectly flush.

Tiling & finish

Every room is set out so cuts sit evenly and edges are crisp. Then the suite, brassware and finishing touches go in with the same care.

The process

Your bathroom, start to finish

A clear plan before we begin, the same familiar faces every day, and a home left clean and tidy at the end.

  1. i.

    Consultation

    We visit your home, talk through ideas and take measurements, then send a detailed written quote.

  2. ii.

    Design & selection

    Layout, tiles, fittings and finishes, chosen together so every element works as a whole.

  3. iii.

    Preparation

    Careful strip-out, then walls, floors and waterproofing prepared properly before any tile goes on.

  4. iv.

    Tiling

    Set out with care so every line, cut and corner is considered.

  5. v.

    Finishing & handover

    Suite and fittings installed, everything checked and cleaned, and your new bathroom handed over ready to enjoy.
